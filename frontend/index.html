How BloodLink Works

Simple, direct, and efficient blood donation management in 3 simple steps

1️⃣

Create an Account

Register as a Blood Donor, Blood Seeker, or Hospital. Set your location and blood group details.

2️⃣

Search & Match

Create emergency blood requests or search for available donors based on city, area, and exact blood compatibility.

3️⃣

Notify & Fulfill

Donors receive instant in-app alerts, accept donation requests, and hospitals mark requests as fulfilled.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can donate blood?

Any healthy adult aged 18-65 weighing at least 50kg who hasn't donated blood in the last 3 months.

Is my personal address exposed?

No. BloodLink only displays your City and Area to protect user privacy.

How do notifications work?

Donors receive in-app alerts whenever a matching emergency request is submitted in their area.
